Application of laser treatment effectively resolves superficial and deep vascular veins.

Trans-cutaneous irradiation prompts blood vessels to absorb photons generating heat which shrinks the blood vessel, eventually dissolving over time.

Additionally, the laser stimulates dermal growth, increasing epidermal thickness and density enhancing skin elasticity thus reducing visibility of small blood vessels.
