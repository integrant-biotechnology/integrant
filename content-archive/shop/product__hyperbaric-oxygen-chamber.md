# Hyperbaric Oxygen Chamber

A monoplace hyperbaric chamber. It is extremely effective chamber with its new generation designs, diameters between 750 and 900 mm, pressure values of 1.5 ATA -2 ATA, acrylic sliding doors, entertainment systems, O2, CO2, humidity, temperature sensors, and air-cooling systems.

Thanks to PLC automated systems, you can record sessions, make validations, use them automatically or manually and end the session safely.

You can use it safely thanks to the internal and external intercom system and safety valves.

With the oxygen concentrator used between 0.8 bar and 3 bar, you can be sure that you can take the most comfortable breath.
